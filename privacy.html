Privacy Policy: Ambience Realms Ops

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Ambience Realms Ops ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Ambience Realms. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's consent, the app uses YouTube API Services with read-only access to:

  • the owner's own channel and video details (YouTube Data API),
  • the owner's own channel statistics and revenue reports (YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API).

The app may also read public data about other channels and videos through the YouTube Data API for market research.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to analyse the channel's performance and plan new videos.
  • Data is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • Data about other channels is deleted within 30 days.
  • The app does not use cookies and does not collect data from viewers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. After that, stored data from the owner's channel is deleted unless access is renewed within 30 days.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: through the channel page or by opening an issue in this site's repository.
